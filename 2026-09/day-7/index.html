The foreman

“Where would a foreman have helped you this weekend?”

3 min demo

4 min of your questions

⏱ 10:15–10:22 · 7 min
🎤 David
  • The foreman is an agent in its own conversation, beside the factory. Before a line runs, it helps you shape the work and starts the line. While it runs, it watches. When the line finishes, it helps you recover what you meant, check you asked for the right thing, and understand the changes well enough to guide the next piece of work.
  • Three minutes is short: show one moment, not the whole loop. Then open it up.
  • Open: which foreman conversation David shows, from which project, and the recorded fallback.

The superpowers workflow

“What would you integrate into your factory?”

3 min of slides

4 min of your questions

⏱ 10:22–10:29 · 7 min
🎤 Aldric (shares his screen)
  • Aldric shares his own screen and slides; come back to this slide for the questions.
  • Superpowers is a skills library for coding agents. In one conversation: brainstorm a design, write a plan, then build it task by task with fresh subagents, test-first, reviewing between tasks.
  • Each step looks like a machine in a factory: the plan is the hand-off, the subagents are the assembly line, review is the validator. The difference: one human conversation steers it all.
