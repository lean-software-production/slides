Total: 90 minutes. Evals were dropped to stop the last class feeling squished. Students start with a word each, then three of them show their capstones. Then each student brings their writing to a troika and gets help from a classmate and a trainer with their next step and its obstacles. Two trainer demos follow: Relay, a working factory, and Memba, Matt's factory on a real project. They close by saying what has changed in their thinking since September. The student-facing agenda has five parts: Show and tell, What's next?, Relay, Memba and Highlight reel. The troika comes straight after the show and tells because it could run long or short; the demos are more flexible, so they absorb the difference. On Tuesday, every talk, demo and show and tell alike, ran about 7 minutes; the demos were slotted for 3, and the questions suffered.

Learning outcomes

  • Say what a classmate built, how, and one thing they learned from it.
  • Describe one thing that worked and one thing that had to change in a trainer's factory, and why.
  • Name their first step in taking the course home, the main obstacle, and one idea from their consultants for getting past it.

Key concepts

  • Troika consulting: in groups of three, each person in turn is the client and asks for help with their challenge; the other two consult while the client listens with their camera off, then the client says what was useful. (Liberating Structures)
